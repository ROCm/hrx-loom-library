# Moonshine AI models

Moonshine packages own their streaming speech-recognition composition, trained
parameter contract, bounded state geometry, and serving controls. The numerical
programs remain model-specific here; extracting reusable kernels into the
library's kernel and motif layers requires preserving their shape, layout, and
independent numerical checks.

[Moonshine Streaming Tiny](moonshine-streaming-tiny/) is the English F32
recognition package. Its portable card selects source or bytecode
providers; deployment chooses the physical device separately.
